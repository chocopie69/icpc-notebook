\section{Polynomials and recurrences}
  Use Polynomial for known coefficients, PolyRoots for real zeros, and PolyInterpolate
  to recover coefficients from sampled values. LinearRecurrence computes a distant sequence
  term modulo an integer; its coefficients describe previous terms, not powers of $x$.
  These snippets are copied separately: Polynomial and LinearRecurrence both name a type
  \texttt{Poly}; rename the latter alias if combining them.
