\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
\item At the start, one teammate sets up the template while the others scan
the problems from opposite ends. Read every statement; solve the easiest first.
\item Track who owns each problem and all submissions. Discuss uncertain
solutions before coding; prepare the algorithm and tricky parts on paper.
\item Share computer time. If stuck, explain the problem to a teammate or
switch tasks; use the scoreboard to spot overlooked easy problems.
\item Before submitting, check bounds, overflow and corner cases. Generate
tests with known answers; ask a teammate to review repeated wrong answers.
\item Near the end, focus the team on the most promising remaining problem:
one codes, one reviews, one builds tests. Leave time to compile and submit.
\end{itemize}
